FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Is soft washing safe for my siding and roof?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure and cleaning solutions that kill mold and algae at the root, so it cleans without forcing water behind siding or stripping shingle granules.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need access to an outside water spigot and any gates. We'll text you before-and-after photos when we finish.

Is there a minimum?

Visits start at $350. Most homes reach that with one or two services, and bundling saves 5 to 10%.

What happens if it rains?

Light rain doesn't affect most cleaning. For heavy rain or freezing temperatures we move you to the next open date at no charge.

How do care plans work?

You pick a plan, we set your spring and fall dates, and you pay monthly or yearly. Plans run 12 months and renew unless you cancel. If you cancel early, you only pay for work already done.

How do I pay?

Card or bank transfer when the job is complete. Care plans use automatic payments.
